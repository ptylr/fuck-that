What the score means

The score measures reluctance to play based entirely on forecast weather. 0–4.9: great. 5–7.9: questionable. 8–10: Fuck That!. It does not rate course quality or difficulty, and it does not tell you whether a course is open or safe. If at least two available forecast models predict severe conditions, the score has a minimum of 9.5, as explained in “How the verdict works”. This is a model-based rule, not an official warning. Check official warnings and the club before travelling.

Weather data

Your browser requests forecasts directly from Open-Meteo using the selected clubs’ coordinates. The provider receives your network IP address and those club locations, which may be near you, not your precise device location or your entered postcode. Provider logs and retention are described in Open-Meteo’s terms and privacy notice.

Open-Meteo data is generally CC BY 4.0. UK Met Office forecast data is CC BY-SA 4.0; the derived forecast blend and weather scores containing that data are offered under CC BY-SA 4.0 as well. We combine available UK Met Office, ECMWF IFS, NOAA GFS and DWD ICON model measurements and calculate the score described in “How the verdict works”. This is a derived planning tool, without endorsement by those providers.

How the verdict works

0 means keen to play. 10 means fuck that. The score is based entirely on forecast weather. 0–4.9: great. 5–7.9: questionable. 8–10: Fuck That!.

Score = (100 − weather quality) ÷ 10. Weather quality combines wind 35%, rain 35%, temperature 15% and feels-like 15%. Calm, dry, 18–24°C is the ideal. We calculate before rounding to one decimal place. Course quality and difficulty do not affect the score.

Four forecast families are requested through Open-Meteo: UK Met Office, ECMWF IFS, NOAA GFS and DWD ICON. Each model supplies measurements for 09:00–16:00 UK time; complete model measurements are averaged before scoring. Some models or days may be unavailable. An explicitly labelled automatic forecast can be used if the multi-model request fails. It counts as one forecast. Models can share inputs; they are not independent safety votes.

At least two forecasts predicting severe conditions apply a minimum score of 9.5: sustained wind ≥30 mph, gusts ≥45 mph, thunder, snow/ice, or feels-like ≤−5°C / ≥38°C. Multiple severe hazards in one forecast count once. A single severe forecast remains visible in the breakdown. Always check official warnings and the club before travelling. We don’t know whether a course is open, waterlogged or on temporary greens.

Days 4–7 are a planning steer; check again nearer tee time. Wind and gusts are model-average daytime maxima. Rain amount is accumulated daytime precipitation; rain chance is the model-average peak hourly probability, not the chance of rain over the whole day. Model disagreement is flagged at a spread of at least 5°C temperature, 10 mph wind or 4 mm precipitation.
